It is important to remember that this game is likely to be short on thumbnail material. Especially since most of the screen will be essentially black during most of the game. For this reason, I intend to place a fairly high amount of effort on getting the HUD and UI in general looking very good. That also goes for the in-game weapon and shield graphics. We deliberately intend to be over the top with these. This is not a simulator, and the rule of cool always trumps realistic a realistic depiction.

With that in mind, here's a word dump that should get the point across:

### Technology

Neon, Vacuum Fluorescent Displays, Cathode Ray Tubes, Soviet Constructivism, Nixie tubes, Vector graphics, holograms.

### Fiction

Star Trek TNG, Star Wars original trilogy, Alien, Tron, Bladerunner

### Phenomena

Lasers, plasma, electric arcs, force fields, tesla coils
